The man trembled as he looked at the pile of firewood and matches in his hands.  Eventually he lit one board, assuring himself that it was just for once.  You see, this firewood was supposed to go towards building his house.  Every day he tried to build, but every time a cold night came, he would tear off a new board and burn it for warmth.  If he had endured the cold a little longer, he could have finished the house and warmed himself inside. Instead, he continued to burn what could save him until there was nothing left.  And therein lies the trap.  Sometimes the quickest way to solve a problem exhausts what is needed to finally solve it.
