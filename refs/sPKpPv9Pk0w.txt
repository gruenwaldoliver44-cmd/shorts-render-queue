They poured a bucket of chocolate powder into the pool, then another, and another.  When the waterfall started to pour, the pool quickly filled with white foam, which looked terrifying. Then they added a second powder to remove the bubbles, creating a chocolate river for the boat to move through. But people started throwing cigarette butts and coffee cups into the river , and hot studio lamps heated it for weeks, causing it to deteriorate and bubble, turning into a smelly mass.
