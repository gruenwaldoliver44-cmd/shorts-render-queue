Pattern (measured, not opinion):
1. Second person, viewer is IN the moment from word 1 ("when you", "if you").
2. One myth or fear set up ("you'd probably think...") then "but".
3. Mechanism in 3-4 causal steps (this -> this triggers -> that).
4. 68-81 words, 25-31 s. Our scripts were 110-145 words, 40-60 s.
5. Zero names, dates, companies, lawsuits. At most one number, saved for the very end.
6. Ends the instant the payoff lands. No "send this to", no question, no twist stack.
