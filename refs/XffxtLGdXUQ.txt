When the lizard approaches, the praying mantis instantly grabs its body with its paw .  And because these paws have rows of sharp spines, they dig deeply into the lizard's skin.  And no matter how much she resists, these spikes hold her firmly in place.  Then the praying mantis pulls it to its mouth. Because its jaws act like little blades, it cuts off piece by piece, eating the lizard alive.
