The fish has a thick growth covering its eyes, and this is done intentionally.  You see, breeders have bred heads large enough to earn points at goldfish shows , but the growth sometimes obscures the fish's view.  Therefore, the fish is taken out of the water and placed in a bowl of anesthetic, where the medication penetrates through the gills, numbing it. It is then placed on a wet table and a tube delivers medicated water into its mouth, sustaining life outside the aquarium while the veterinarian cuts off the growth so the fish can see again.
