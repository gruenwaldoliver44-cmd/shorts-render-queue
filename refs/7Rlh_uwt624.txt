They pulled out a butterfly's antennae, connected it to a circuit, and then blew floral scent on it .  And as soon as he smelled the smell, the cells activated , responding faster than odor sensors designed for the same job.  So, they connected it to a computer that could read the circuit before transmitting those signals to a navigation program .  Then they mounted it on a palm-sized drone.  And when it smelled a smell, the program would read the signal and then direct the drone towards that source of the smell. With proper training, this thing could sniff out survivors of a disaster.
