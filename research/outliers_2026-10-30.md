# Viral Short-Form Outliers: Script-Template Reference
Compiled 2026-10-30. Sources were limited to WebSearch and WebFetch.

## How to read this file
- **VERIFIED**: a view count of 1M or more that I read on a third-party page, named in the entry. Counts are snapshots from that page's date, and live counts are usually higher.
  - pickscribe.com is a YouTube transcript mirror that shows view counts.
  - outlierkit.com is a YouTube analytics site.
  - Press articles cite TikTok counts.
- **PARTIAL**: the video exists and the caption or transcript is verified, but the view count comes from a self-published source or only a lower bound is known.
- **UNVERIFIED**: the URL and caption came from search results. I could not confirm a view count. Treat the entry as a lead only.
- **Hook source**: says whether the hook is the spoken opener (from a transcript), the on-screen caption or title, or a quote reported by an article. Nothing here was invented. A missing hook is marked "not found".
- **Format flag**: some verified YouTube items are long-form. They prove the topic and hook but not the vertical format. Each one is labelled.

## Access limits that shaped this list
- youtube.com watch and Shorts pages returned bot-protection shells with no view data.
- TikTok, Instagram, X, Playboard, Social Blade and fandom wikis refused the fetch.
- Exact Shorts counts came only from pickscribe channel pages, which covered just a few channels.
- Most TikTok counts come from news articles.
- I could not resolve the accounts so.thats.how and scroll.engineer to any indexable page. No numbers for them could be verified.

## Cross-niche patterns worth copying (from the verified items)
1. **Second-person scenario in the first 3 seconds** ("when you pump gas…", "if you're trapped in the trunk…", "if you and your roommate order the exact same…"). The verified 70M+ Machine Times shorts all start inside a moment the viewer knows.
2. **"What [brand] doesn't want you to know"** is the single most proven Compound Times frame. Erika Kullberg passed 5M with it on at least 5 brands, and her top 3 reached 27M, 40M and 46–75M.
3. **Familiar view, then impossible view** (Jared Owen: "you've seen this view… but have you seen this view?"). This is the native 3D hook and maps directly onto Blender cutaways.
4. **Fee stacking and line-item forensics** (DoorDash $6.50 to $22.93, the mystery $8 barcode) can be rendered as a 3D receipt that builds line by line.
5. **Exact giant numbers in titles** (Toyota's "$2,300,000,000 Mistake", "$300M for 2 pizzas") for Money Classics and Future Tender.
