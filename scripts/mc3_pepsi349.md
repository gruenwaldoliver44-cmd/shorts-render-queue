## Script (~116 words)
1. This bottle cap was worth 40,000 dollars. So were 800,000 others.
2. Philippines, 1992. Pepsi announced the winning number: 349.
3. One winning cap was over 50 years of an average salary.
4. All together: 32 billion dollars.
5. Pepsi said those caps lacked a security code, and offered 18 dollars each.
6. Almost half a million took it.
7. Then Pepsi trucks were stoned and burned.
8. A bomb killed a teacher and a five-year-old.
9. A grenade killed three Pepsi workers. Who was behind the attacks was never confirmed.
10. About 22,000 took legal action. In 2006, the Supreme Court said Pepsi didn't have to pay.
11. 32 billion on paper. Pepsi paid out about 9 million.
12. Would you have taken the 18 dollars?
