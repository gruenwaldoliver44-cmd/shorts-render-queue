There's an asteroid made of metal worth ten thousand quadrillion dollars, and NASA has already sent a spaceship to it. It's called Psyche, and it might be the exposed core of a dead planet. The mission's lead scientist came up with that number herself, and then called it meaningless. Because if that much metal ever reached Earth, gold, iron and platinum would stop being rare. The spaceship arrives in twenty twenty-nine. And the most valuable rock in the solar system could make the gold in your jewelry box worth almost nothing.
