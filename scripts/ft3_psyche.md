# FT3 · 16 Psyche (template: "If/Imagine" opener + twist ending)
Imagine a rock in space worth more than all the money on Earth, many times over. It's called Psyche, and it's made mostly of metal. The mission's lead scientist once put its value at ten thousand quadrillion dollars. And a NASA spacecraft is already on its way to study it. It arrives in twenty twenty-nine. But here's the thing. She called that number meaningless. Because if that much metal ever reached Earth, metal would stop being rare. And every gold ring and gold bar down here could lose most of its value.
Facts: Elkins-Tanton 2017 estimate $10,000 quadrillion, called "essentially meaningless"; Psyche launched Oct 2023, Mars flyby May 2026, arrival 2029 (JPL, Wikipedia). Last sentence is labelled speculation on screen.
