## Script (~97 words, ~37 s)
1. If you peel off that "warranty void if removed" sticker, your warranty is still valid.
2. In the US, a 1975 law says companies can't make you use their own parts or repairs to keep your warranty.
3. That's why the FTC warned Sony, Microsoft, Nintendo… and even Hyundai.
4. Because this covers your car too.
5. Get your oil changed anywhere. Keep the receipt. The dealer still has to honor your warranty.
6. One catch: if the outside repair caused the damage, that part isn't covered.
7. Send this to someone who still pays the dealer for every oil change.

## Facts
- FTC warning letters April 2018 to six companies: Sony, Microsoft, Nintendo, Hyundai, HTC, ASUS (names via Motherboard FOIA), 30 days to fix (Vice/Motherboard; NPR Apr 11 2018).
- Magnuson-Moss Warranty Act 1975: can't condition warranty on use of particular brand articles/services unless free (FTC).
- FTC consumer guidance: dealer can't deny warranty simply because maintenance/repairs were done elsewhere; damage caused by the outside repair can be excluded.
