# CT5 — Scratch ticket singletons (Mohan Srivastava, Toronto 2003)
Sources: WIRED 'Cracking the Scratch Lottery Code' (Jan 2011); Fox News/AP (Feb 2011): 3 singletons in a row -> winner 9/10; reported to OLG; game pulled next day.
Script (Mark, 30.0 s):
There's a way to know if a scratch ticket is a winner… before you scratch it. In two thousand three, a geologist in Toronto picked up a tic-tac-toe ticket. You see, every ticket shows rows of printed numbers, right there on the front. And he noticed some numbers appear only once. So he started circling them. When three of those lonely numbers lined up in a row… the ticket won nine times out of ten. He could have cleaned out every store in town. Instead, he called the lottery. And the game was gone the next day.
