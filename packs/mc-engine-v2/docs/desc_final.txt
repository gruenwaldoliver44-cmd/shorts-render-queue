What happens to your money if the AI bubble bursts and crashes the whole world economy? Is your money still worth anything, which country comes out on top, and who writes the new rules afterwards? An animated what-if scenario, step by step, from the first missed payment to the new world order.

We follow one made-up company, Corvane, and every step after it has really happened before: Tokyo's 2024 crash, gold's 2026 drop, Egypt's bread crisis, Silicon Valley Bank, the 1931 Creditanstalt panic, the 1929 rally, the 1933 gold order and Bretton Woods 1944.

This is a scenario. Corvane is fictional. The historical facts and 2026 figures are real.
