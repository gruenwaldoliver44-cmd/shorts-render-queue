Same bank. Same fee. Completely different treatment. We follow Jess, a new bank teller who just got hit with a $35 overdraft fee herself, through one day at the counter as customers from every level of wealth walk up to her window: from $59 in the red to $50 million in the bank.

You'll see who actually pays for the red carpet at the top, why a florist gets her fee refunded in ten seconds while Jess gets a recording, why a millionaire gets paid to switch banks, how a $10 million client borrows cheaper than you, and the one setting on your phone that stops the most common overdraft fee.
